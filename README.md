Entry for the [news-image-20261122 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122) on make.fun: sum up 22 November's world news in one image.

36 stories, 7 regions, every one dated Sunday 22 November 2026. The front page carries the date as its headline, six key numbers, a region strip, a "Day in UTC" timeline, three lead cards with large drawn scenes, ten smaller cards with drawn scene banners, a dotted world map with numbered pins, a 23-story wire list, and a DATE CHECK box naming the big stories left out because they belong to 20–21 November.

`render.py` (Python 3, Pillow) draws `entry.png` (1024×1024) from `stories_20261122.json`. `main.ts` (Node 22) copies the PNG to the output directory.
